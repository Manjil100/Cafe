It started with a cinnamon roll

Back when he was cooking in Rotorua, our chef-owner started every day the same way: a warm cinnamon roll and a flat white from a little bakery on the way to work.

Moving to Tauranga meant leaving that morning ritual behind. Hey Jude is how he brought it back, for everyone. The cinnamon rolls are made here, the coffee comes from Sacred Grounds in Taupō, and the kitchen cooks proper food all day.
