Your details

How Hey Jude handles what you give us when you book a table or send a message.

What we collect

When you book, we ask for your name, phone number, the date and time you want, how many people are coming, and any notes you add.

When you send a message, we ask for your name, email address, what it’s about and the message itself.

How we use it

  • Only to hold your table, or to reply to your message.
  • We don’t sell or share it, and we don’t send marketing.
  • Only signed-in café staff can see bookings and messages.

Cookies

This website doesn’t use tracking or advertising cookies, so there’s no cookie banner. The map on the Visit page comes from Google Maps and only loads when you choose to show it. Google may set its own cookies once it does.

Seeing, correcting or deleting your details

Send us a message and we’ll show you what we hold, fix it, or delete it. This follows the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.
