About Hey Jude

A Brookfield café that started with one good morning routine.

Where it started

Before Hey Jude, our chef-owner was cooking in Rotorua. Every morning on the way to work he stopped at a little bakery for a warm cinnamon roll and a flat white, and ate it outside in the sun.

When he moved to Tauranga he missed that start to the day, so he built a café around it. The cinnamon rolls are baked here, the coffee is taken seriously, and the kitchen cooks from open to close.

“It was a simple pleasure, but it meant the world to me.”
Hey Jude’s owner

Coffee from Sacred Grounds

Every cup is made with beans from Sacred Grounds, roasters in Taupō. Have it as a flat white with a cinnamon roll, the way it all started.
