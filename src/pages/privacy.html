How Hey Jude handles what you give us when you book a table.

What we collect

When you book, we ask for your name, phone number, the date and time you want, how many people are coming, and any notes you add.

How we use it

  • Only to hold your table and to contact you about that booking.
  • We don’t sell or share it, and we don’t send marketing.
  • Only signed-in café staff can see bookings.

Cookies

This website doesn’t use tracking or advertising cookies.
